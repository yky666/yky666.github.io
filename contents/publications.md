### Papers Under Review

- **Beyond Actions: Learning Future Operation Targets for Vision-Language-Action Models**<br>
  Min Lin, Bokai Zhou, Jianqi Lin, **Kaiyuan Yang**, Yicheng Chang, Junjie Yu, Xiaofan Liu, Jianhua Han, Rui Li, Shen Zhao, Xiaodan Liang.<br>
  **Submitted to ICLR 2027 · Fourth author.**<br>
  Studies future operation representations for vision-language-action policies through Action–Future Coupled Effect Learning (AFCE), with evaluations on dexterous manipulation tasks.

- **Efficient-RLT: Sample-efficient Real-robot RL via Optimizing Human-intervention Strategy with Dense Process Rewards**<br>
  Jian Zhang, Weijia Liufu, Xiaoyu Guo, Ziang Yan, Bingchang Song, **Kaiyuan Yang**, et al.<br>
  **Submitted to ICLR 2027 · Sixth author.**<br>
  Studies sample-efficient real-robot reinforcement learning using rewind interventions, pairwise preference learning, and dense progress rewards.
